Final idea:
Instead of searching all possible solutions and then picking one, pick a potential solution at random
and then test it and subsequent positions as necessary until a valid placement is found.

Performance:
Worst case is an exhaustive search of all positions.
This is unlikely as a wordsearch packed tightly enough to achieve it is so densely packed with words
that finding them becomes almost trivially easy.

Best case is quiteg good, with typical cases likely only checking a handful of positions.

Drawback: interesting positions (intersections) are often overlooked.

Middle-ground: try a percentage (perhaps 10%) of all possible placements at random and pick the best.
Will require experimenting.
